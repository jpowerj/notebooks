Though [[The_World/Namibia]] was originally "granted" to [[The_World/Apartheid_South_Africa]] by the [[United Nations]] as a [[mandate]], after years of being a colony of [[The_World/Germany]] (years which included the [[Herero Genocide]]), [[The_World/SWAPO]] fought alongside the [[The_World/African National Congress]] and [[The_World/Cuba]] to finally secure independence in 1990, after the [[Battle of Cuito Cuanavale]] in 1988.
