Though [[Namibia]] was originally "granted" to [[Apartheid_South_Africa]] by the [[United Nations]] as a [[mandate]], after years of being a colony of [[Germany]] (years which included the [[Herero Genocide]]), [[SWAPO]] fought alongside the [[African National Congress]] and [[Cuba]] to finally secure independence in 1990, after the [[Battle of Cuito Cuanavale]] in 1988.
