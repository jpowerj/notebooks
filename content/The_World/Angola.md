I spent a *lot* of time trying to wrap my mind around Angola for the dissertation, since it's basically where [[The_World/Cuba]] got the ball rolling in terms of driving [[Apartheid]] out of [[The_World/Southern_Africa]]. Basically, when the dust settled from the [[First Angolan Civil War]] with the [[MPLA]] in control over most of the country[^1], it became a forward base from which Angolan and [[The_World/Cuba\|Cuban]] troops could (a) defend against [[SADF]] incursions, (b) train fighters from [[The_World/Southern_Africa\|neighboring countries]], and then (c) launch joint attacks against, for example, South African troops in [[The_World/Occupied_Namibia\|Occupied Namibia]].

# Footnotes

[^1]: In contrast to [[The_World/Zimbabwe]], where the China-backed group, [[The_World/ZANU]], came to power over the Soviet-backed [[The_World/ZAPU]].
